How filtering works

  • Jev answers one yes-or-no question per block, in a single call.
  • Don't show wins: a match hides the post, even if it also matches Show.
  • Show is a whitelist: while it has content, posts matching none of it are hidden.
  • Hidden posts stay in place — use show to reveal one.
